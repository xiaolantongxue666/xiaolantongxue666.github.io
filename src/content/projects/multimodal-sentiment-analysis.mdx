<section class="research-section" id="data" aria-labelledby="data-title">
  <header class="research-section-heading">
    <span class="research-section-label">01 / data engineering</span>
    <h2 id="data-title">先对齐时间，<br />再融合信息。</h2>
    <p>将异构媒体转换为可追溯、带有效性掩码的统一时序输入。</p>
  </header>
  <div class="research-section-body">
    <p>基于 <strong>CMU-MOSEI</strong> 数据，构建覆盖 <strong>100 条原始视频</strong>的处理流程。文本、语音和视觉映射至统一的 <strong>0.2 秒时间网格</strong>，保存有效性掩码及原始素材对应关系。</p>
    <dl class="research-spec">
      <div><dt>source videos</dt><dd>100 条</dd></div>
      <div><dt>temporal resolution</dt><dd>0.2 秒</dd></div>
    </dl>
    <div class="research-methods">
      <div class="research-method" data-modality="vision">
        <span class="research-method-mark" aria-hidden="true">[V]</span>
        <div><h3>从视频帧提取视觉信号</h3><p>利用 OpenCV 完成人脸区域检测，提取 HOG、HSV 颜色及光流运动特征，覆盖形状、颜色和运动信息。</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="research-method">
        <span class="research-method-mark" aria-hidden="true">[A]</span>
        <div><h3>词级对齐与时间映射</h3><p>结合 Wav2Vec2-CTC 词级对齐，将三模态信息映射至共同时间轴，为跨模态交互提供对应关系。</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="research-method" data-modality="mask">
        <span class="research-method-mark" aria-hidden="true">[M]</span>
        <div><h3>保留有效性与素材索引</h3><p>保存有效性掩码，标记输入的可用状态；保留特征与原始素材的对应关系，支持后续关键文本、语音时段和视频帧定位。</p></div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="research-section" id="architecture" aria-labelledby="architecture-title">
  <header class="research-section-heading">
    <span class="research-section-label">02 / model architecture</span>
    <h2 id="architecture-title">感知缺失，<br />学习模态交互。</h2>
    <p>BERT 掩码感知门控交互网络，联合完成情感分类与强度回归。</p>
  </header>
  <div class="research-section-body">
    <p>使用 <strong>BERT 文本编码</strong>、<strong>Transformer 跨模态交互</strong>及<strong>自适应门控融合</strong>，将文本、语音和视觉信息汇入联合预测流程。</p>
    <figure class="research-architecture">
      <figcaption>模型计算流程 · 架构示意</figcaption>
      <div class="research-inputs">
        <div class="research-node" data-modality="text"><span class="research-node-code">text</span><h3>BERT 编码</h3><p>文本语义</p></div>
        <div class="research-node" data-modality="audio"><span class="research-node-code">audio</span><h3>语音输入</h3><p>词级时间对齐</p></div>
        <div class="research-node" data-modality="vision"><span class="research-node-code">vision</span><h3>视觉特征</h3><p>HOG / HSV / 光流</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="research-flow-arrow" aria-hidden="true">↓</div>
      <div class="research-node"><h3>Transformer 跨模态交互</h3><p>建模文本、语音与视觉之间的交互关系</p></div>
      <div class="research-flow-arrow" aria-hidden="true">↓</div>
      <div class="research-node research-node-fusion"><h3>掩码感知 · 自适应门控融合</h3><p>有效性掩码 + 多模态信息</p></div>
      <div class="research-flow-arrow" aria-hidden="true">↓</div>
      <div class="research-outputs">
        <div class="research-node"><span class="research-node-code">classification</span><h3>情感三分类</h3></div>
        <div class="research-node"><span class="research-node-code">regression</span><h3>情感强度回归</h3></div>
      </div>
      <p class="research-architecture-foot">三模态输入统一至 0.2 秒时间网格；图中展示主要模块与任务关系。</p>
    </figure>
    <div class="research-methods">
      <div class="research-method"><span class="research-method-mark" aria-hidden="true">[×]</span><div><h3>连续区间缺失增强</h3><p>在连续时段模拟局部信息缺失，处理不同模态组合、缺失比例及缺失位置，评估模型对不完整输入的适应能力。</p></div></div>
    </div>
    <details class="research-disclosure">
      <summary>为什么同时保留对齐、掩码和原始素材关系？</summary>
      <p>时间对齐建立模态间的对应；有效性掩码保留输入是否可用的信息；原始素材关系使解释结果能够回到具体文本、语音时段和视频帧。三者分别服务于交互建模、缺失处理与证据追溯。</p>
    </details>
  </div>
</section>
